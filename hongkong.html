The week at a glance

Busy days, then slow ones

Seven days in Hong Kong over the busiest week of its year, then three days of markets and massages in Saigon. The beach and Disneyland fall on working days, New Year's Eve afternoon is kept free for a nap, New Year's Day is slow, and the Saigon days mix shopping with long rests.

Southside, Hong Kong Island

  1. 10:30 AM

    Repulse Bay Beach 淺水灣

    Late December is mild by Hong Kong standards: highs around 20°C, and the sea is about the same. Think a long walk on the sand and a coffee facing the water rather than a swim. If you do want a dip, Deep Water Bay along the Seaview Promenade has lifeguards all winter.

    Where109 Repulse Bay Road · The Pulse, 28 Beach Road

  3. 2:30 PM

    An early dinner near the hotel and a proper night's sleep before Disneyland.

No lifeguards at Repulse Bay in winter. They're off duty from December to February. Neighbouring Deep Water Bay is staffed through the winter, 8 AM–5 PM in past seasons.
迪士尼

Day 4 · Wednesday 30 December

Disneyland at Christmas

Lantau Island

  1. Opening

    Be at the gates before the park opens 香港迪士尼樂園

    "A Disney Christmas" runs from 20 November 2026 to 3 January 2027 and is included with admission. Wednesday is usually the park's quietest weekday, but the week between Christmas and New Year is one of its busiest, so the first hour still matters most. Go straight to World of Frozen, then work back toward Main Street.

    Getting thereMTR to Sunny Bay then change to the Disneyland Resort line · about 30 minutes from Hong Kong or Kowloon station

  2. 12:00 PM

    Lunch at Plaza Inn

    A Chinese table-service restaurant on Main Street with vegetarian dishes marked on the menu. Reserve online; walk-in waits will be long this week. For something quick, Starliner Diner in Tomorrowland has a veggie burger.

  3. Afternoon

    Christmas shows, then a break

    Mid-afternoon is the busiest stretch. Use it for the Christmas entertainment and a hot drink so you have energy left for the evening.

  4. Closing

    Momentous nighttime spectacular

    About 20 minutes of fireworks and projections over the castle. Find a spot on Main Street early. Check the park calendar for the showtime on the 30th.

Book the ticket and the date together, now. Every guest needs a park reservation for a specific day, and each ticket type has a daily quota that sells out in holiday weeks. Tickets are priced in four tiers by date; check the official calendar for the 30th. The park also holds its own New Year's Eve countdown at the castle, if you'd rather spend the 31st there.

Eating vegetarian

The vegetarian table

Hong Kong is easier for vegetarians than it looks, as long as you know where meat and fish hide. At ordinary Cantonese restaurants, stir-fried greens usually come with oyster sauce, and chicken powder and dried shrimp turn up as background seasoning. Dedicated vegetarian restaurants, marked 齋 or 素, remove the guesswork. In holiday week, book every dinner. In Saigon the hidden ingredient is fish sauce, which goes into almost everything, so stick to quán chay (vegetarian eateries) and use the Vietnamese phrases below.

Two shopping days

What to buy where

Hong Kong has no sales tax, so the price on the tag is the price you pay. In Hong Kong, the morning of New Year's Eve covers Kowloon's markets and specialist streets, and New Year's Day afternoon covers the Island's boutiques and malls. Saigon gets two full market days on 4 and 5 January. Leave room in your VietJet baggage allowance for all of it.
